Privacy

          
Prismagram has no server and sends no telemetry. Your schema stays on your computer; the only connections are the update check and the databases you open.

          

            
Clear all dataCloses open connections and removes saved connections, their passwords and your settings. Your files are not touched.

Import from Supabase

        
Reads the tables, columns, enums and foreign keys, and writes them as a Prisma schema. Only the structure is read: no rows, and nothing is changed.


        
        
        
In Supabase open your project, click Connect and copy the Session pooler string. Any PostgreSQL URL works.


        
        

        
        
        
Comma-separated. Add auth to include Supabase's users table.


        


        

          The password is never saved.
          
          
        

      

    

    

    
      

        
New connection

        
PostgreSQL, Supabase and MongoDB (including Atlas). The connection stays open until you disconnect it.


        
        

        
        
        

          For example postgresql://user:pass@host:5432/db or mongodb://localhost:27017/app.
          A whole DATABASE_URL=… line from .env works too.
